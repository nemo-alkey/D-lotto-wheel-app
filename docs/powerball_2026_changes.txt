Powerball, NZ's favourite game has changed


How do the Powerball changes affect me?
 
From Sunday 13 September we added four new Powerballs to the draw machine. This means bigger prizes, bigger jackpots, more chances to win and changes to the odds. Here's what you need to know about the changes.


More winners
More winners each week with the introduction of a brand new Powerball Division 8 cash prize of $12.


Bigger prizes
Prizes have increased across all Powerball divisions. On average you could take home more than with the former 10-ball Powerball game.


Bigger Powerball jackpots
The Powerball starting jackpot has increased from $4 million to $5 million. The maximum jackpot has increased from $50 million to $60 million.


Why has Powerball changed?

Lotto NZ exists to provide funding to a wide range of community causes, including grassroots sports, cultural groups and health organisations. All our profits go to the Lottery Grants Board for distribution to communities across New Zealand, more than $6.7 billion since 1987!  These game changes help us to keep supporting important community causes now and well into the future.
 

The update also keeps the Powerball game fresh and exciting, while retaining much of what our players already love about Powerball. 


How have the Powerball odds changed?

The addition of four new Powerballs means the odds of winning in all the existing 7 Powerball divisions has changed (it is now harder to win a prize in existing divisions). For Powerball Division 1, the odds per line have changed from 1 in 38.4 million to 1 in 53.7 million per line on your Powerball ticket. See the full odds (pdf), including the updated Powerball odds that took effect from 13 September.
 

The odds for playing Lotto and other Lotto NZ games have not changed.


What if I have a Powerball subscription?

Active Powerball Subscriptions will continue without you having to do anything. However, since four new Powerball numbers (11–14) have been added, you might like to review your Subscriptions to update the Powerball numbers you are playing.
 

If you want to do this, and want your updates in place for the first Powerball draw under the new rules on Wednesday 16 September, you will have to make the changes to your subscription before 10pm Monday 14 September.

 
 
Powerball changes FAQ

When did these changes happen?
The final Powerball draw under the current game rules was on Saturday 12 September 2026. 

The new game rules came into effect from Sunday 13 September 2026, with the first draw under the new rules taking place on Wednesday 16 September 2026.


Have Powerball ticket prices increased?
No, Powerball ticket prices have stayed the same. Whether you play a Dip or Pick Your Own, it doesn’t cost any extra. The change means there are simply more Powerball numbers to choose from.


How has Powerball changed since it was introduced in 2001?
Since launching in 2001, Powerball has evolved to create bigger jackpots and more winners:

2007: Two balls were added to Powerball, to increase prize amounts.  The starting jackpot rose from $1 million to $3 million, and the maximum jackpot increased to $30 million.
 
2010: A price change helped grow prizes further, with the starting jackpot increasing to $4 million.
 
2015: Powerball expanded to two draws per week. Changes to prize divisions, including the introduction of Powerball Division 7, meant more chances to win. The maximum jackpot increased to $40 million.
 
2017: Updates to Lotto and Powerball pricing boosted prizes across all divisions. Lotto Division 7 was introduced, creating even more winners, and the Powerball jackpot maximum increased to $50 million.  
 
2026: Four new Powerballs were added on September 13. Powerball Division 8 was introduced, creating even more winners, and the Powerball starting jackpot went from a $4 Million jackpot to a $5 Million one, and the maximum Powerball jackpot increased to $60 million.
