## Wheel Definitions

| Name | Tickets | Pool | Guarantee |
|------|---------|------|-----------|
| `single1` | 20 | 10 | 4‑win if 4 pool numbers drawn |
| `single2` | 20 | 10 | 4‑win if 4 pool numbers drawn |
| `double` | 88 | 10 | Two 4‑wins if 4 pool numbers drawn |
| `five-if-six` | 11 | 11 | 5‑win if all 6 drawn from pool |
| `jackpot7` | 7 | 7 | Jackpot (6‑win) if all 6 drawn from pool |

Custom wheels can be generated via `wheel_generator.py` with configurable pool size, guarantee, bonus coverage, and include/exclude numbers.

## Prize Calculation (Lotto Rules 2025)

The `prize_calculator.py` module implements the official NZ Lotto Rules 2025. For Standard Lotto, the prize pool is split into percentages after deducting the reserve fund (5%) and fixed Div7 prizes. Powerball uses a 94.72% allocation for Div1, capped at $50M. Jackpot rollover is supported with forced distribution after 10 consecutive rollovers.

**Reserve fund rates**: Lotto 5%, Powerball 10%, Strike 3%
